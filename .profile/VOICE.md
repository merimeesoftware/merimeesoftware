## Tone
- Short sentences, first person, energetic, specific
- Evidence over assertion; never invent titles, numbers, or dates
- Big beats over task bullets; outcomes people can picture
- No badge soup, emoji walls, or “Hi I’m …” fluff
- No “aspiring.” No “passionate.” No hype words. No salary, household, or internal politics.

## Prefer
- “I build AI that does real work.”
- “A person is accountable for every change.”
- “A tool is good when it serves the person using it.”
- Identity, gates, audit, blast radius
- Live URL over private repo link

## Avoid
- Any “open to,” “seeking,” or “looking for” line. No job-search announcement.
- Naming the typesetting product, its working names, or its domain. Call it the book typesetting studio. No link.
- Linking private repos. Private work is named with one line each until links are ready.
- Opening the story with teaching or anything before Scepter. Making hard ideas simple can appear as a trait.
- Closed personal projects, household, and finances (the private source lists them)
- Scepter percentages until Michael approves them
- Counts of agents, frameworks, skills, or templates; start dates for AI work
- Numbers outside the public resume set
- Programming languages until confirmed
- Headlining Solutions Engineer, sales-quota SE, or high-travel FDE
- Crowding into “security engineer” as the identity
- Internal system names, colleague names, proprietary topology, org politics
- Dead client links
- Archived repos (Elyra, templates) on the public front
- X/Twitter unless explicitly re-enabled
